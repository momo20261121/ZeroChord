## Full description

```text
Make music with your CardputerZero keyboard. Play a chord with one hand and pick or strum its notes with the other. Combine Chord, Arp, and Pad backing layers, or play Front only. Nine editable presets let you choose sounds for Front, Chord/Arp, and Pad and adjust envelopes, tempo, and patterns. Customize key mappings, shape the mix with three-band EQ and Echo, or import SoundFont packs from a TF card.

Make your first sound
1. Press 8 for a C major chord. The app starts silently until you play.
2. Play Z X C V B N M to pick its notes. L strums up. K strums down.
3. Try 8 → 5 → T → 4 for C → G → Am → F in the default key of C.
4. Press O to choose backing layers (Chord, Arp, Pad, or none for Front only). I toggles Hold.
5. Press Space to stop sound. Hold Esc to return to the launcher.

Inspired by minichord (https://minichord.com/) and the Suzuki Omnichord (https://www.suzuki-music.co.jp/special/omnichord_om-108/en/).
User guide (https://github.com/momo20261121/ZeroChord/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md)
Source on GitHub (https://github.com/momo20261121/ZeroChord)
```
